Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 4, 2026

Welcome to Grandatlas Games & Apps. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information processed through our mobile applications and games (including Z Dodge, EarnHubCall, and future releases).

1. Information We Collect

Our games and applications are designed to be enjoyed without forcing personal registration or user account creation. However, third-party frameworks integrated into our apps may collect standard non-personally identifiable technical information:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID).
  • Usage & Gameplay Data: High scores, game progress, session duration, and crash logs to improve stability and performance.

2. Third-Party Advertising & Services

To keep our games free to play, we may display third-party advertisements (such as Google AdMob). These advertising networks may use cookies or advertising IDs to serve personalized or contextual ads.

You can learn more about how Google handles advertising data by reviewing the Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Local Data Storage

Game preferences, unlocked items, and highest scores are stored directly on your local device storage. Deleting or clearing the app data will reset this locally saved progress.

4. Children's Privacy

Our products do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

5. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated effective date.
